# 16. Legal and Operations

This chapter lists what must be in place before launch. It is not legal advice; confirm each point with a lawyer and a chartered accountant.

## Company and tax

- **Company registration** in India (for example a private limited company or LLP) to sign store agreements and receive payouts.
- **GST registration**. In-game purchases carry **18% GST**.

## Online gaming law

- **Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025** (India): online money games are banned. GANGS OF BHARAT is a social/casual game with **no cash-out of anything**: coins, ₹, chips, items and accounts can never be converted to real money or prizes. The casino uses chips bought with in-game ₹ only.
- Real-money trading of accounts or items is against the ToS and leads to a permanent ban.

## Privacy and terms

- **DPDP Act, 2023** (Digital Personal Data Protection): collect the minimum data, get consent, explain use, allow deletion requests, protect data. Only the Owner can see players' real info.
- **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** in Hindi and English, shown at sign-up and in the store listings.
- **CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines, 2023** for the store (see [11. Monetization](11-monetization.md)).
- **Refund policy** in line with Google Play and App Store rules.
- **18+ age gate** at first launch, plus the correct age rating in both stores.

## Trademarks

- **Trademark check** for "GANGS OF BHARAT" before launch.
- All vehicle and product brands in the game are parodies (Marutha, Mahindar, Tatva, Ranga Rover, Mercedez, BMV, Lambor-Gini, Ferraro, parody cigarettes and pan masala).
