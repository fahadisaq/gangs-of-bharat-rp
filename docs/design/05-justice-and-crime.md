# 05. Justice and Crime

## Wanted level

- Wanted level **1 to 5** stars. Higher levels mean more police alerts, a bigger fine and a longer jail term.
- Crimes add wanted stars (theft, assault, illegal weapon, robbery, unfair kill, escape).
- Wanted players are shown to on-duty police; **wanted posters** appear in the city for high levels.

## Jail

- Jail time **5 to 30 minutes** of play time, based on charges and the verdict.
- **Jail jobs** (cleaning, kitchen, laundry) let prisoners earn a little ₹ and pass time.
- Logging out does not reduce jail time.

## Court flow (unfair kill and serious crimes)

1. **Unfair kill** (or another serious crime) -> **automatic police alert** and **wanted level** for the attacker.
2. Police **arrest** the suspect.
3. Police file **charges with evidence** (logs, witness statements, recordings from the report system).
4. The suspect gets a **lawyer** (a licensed civilian lawyer, or the government defender as fallback).
5. **Trial with a real judge** (a player in the Court faction). The prosecutor presents the case.
6. **Verdict**: fine, jail time, license loss, or acquittal.
7. **One appeal** to a different judge or the Chief Justice.

## Lawyer job

- Lawyer is a **licensed civilian job**. The license is obtained through a law exam at the court.
- Lawyers take clients, charge fees and represent them at trial. Fee rates are set during balancing.
- A **government defender** (court role) is the fallback when no lawyer is available.

## Weapons

**Confirmed by the owner:** weapons, including guns, are in the game. The game is rated 18+ (see [01. Vision and Scope](01-vision-and-scope.md) and [16. Legal and Operations](16-legal-ops.md)).

- **Weapons are rare and serious.** A gun is never a casual item: getting one is hard, and using one has heavy consequences (wanted stars, arrest, court, injury).
- **Legal guns:** only **Police and Army**, with a **weapon licence**, and only while on duty. Guns are issued from the faction armory (see [04. Factions](04-factions.md)).
- **Illegal guns:** gangs (and everyone else without a licence) can only get guns illegally, through **smuggling** and **convoy theft** (see the weapon convoy in [06. Events](06-events.md)), plus the black market and mafia arms sales. Carrying an illegal weapon is a crime.
- Weapons cannot be used in safe zones (places of worship) or by players under new-player protection.

## Character Kill (CK)

**DRAFT – owner review**: a Character Kill (permanent death of a character) happens **only with the player's agreement**. Process and approvals are not final.

## Combat logging

Leaving the game to avoid arrest, injury or a fight is **combat logging** and is **punished**. The character stays in the world for a short time after disconnecting during combat or while wanted, and admins get an automatic alert.

## Roleplay rules

- **No RDM** (random deathmatch): no attacking without an RP reason.
- **No metagaming**: no using information from outside the game (streams, Discord) inside RP.
- **No powergaming**: no forcing actions on others or doing impossible things.
- **NLR** (new life rule): after being revived at the hospital, the player forgets the events that led to the knockdown and does not return to that scene right away.
- **Value of life**: act as if your life matters; do not ignore a gun pointed at you.

Breaking RP rules follows the punishment ladder in [12. Admin and Moderation](12-admin-moderation.md).

## Contraband

Gangs trade **generic contraband** (unnamed illegal goods) instead of drugs. **DRAFT – owner review**: drugs are not in the game; whether generic contraband is the final approach.

## Bribery

Bribery between players and police/officials is possible as RP and is **logged on the server** so admins can review abuse. **DRAFT – owner review**: whether bribery is allowed and its exact rules.

## Black market

- The **black market moves to a new location every day**. Its location spreads by rumors, informers and gang contacts.
- Sells illegal weapons, lockpicks, drills and contraband at high prices.

## Informers and undercover police

- **Informers**: players can sell information to the police (for example a gang hideout or a planned robbery).
- **Undercover police**: selected police members can work in plain clothes. Their actions are logged and visible to admins.
- **Wanted posters** with rewards for high-level wanted players.
