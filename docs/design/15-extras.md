# 15. Extras

All items below are part of the design. They ship in the full release unless the roadmap moves them.

## Progression and rewards

- Levels and XP
- Daily rewards
- Achievements
- Leaderboards
- Skills (driving, shooting, stamina, cooking and more)

## Social

- Relationships and weddings (5★ hotel wedding hall)
- Crews (player groups with crew chat)
- Pets
- Nightlife (bars, clubs, music)
- Phone social app
- Referrals
- Emotes

## Server and account

- VIP queue (VIP members join a full server faster)
- **No wipes**

## AI features (Sarvam)

- AI talking NPCs
- AI news anchor
- Auto-translate chat (Hindi <-> English)
- AI voice moderation
- AI side missions

## Gameplay activities

- Heists
- Detective system (investigations with clues and evidence)
- Stock market
- Bollywood studio (film shoots, acting jobs)
- Cricket (matches and screenings)
- Live events

## Creators and community

- Streamer mode (hides names, chat and personal data on screen)
- Creator codes
- Clip recording
- Discord
- Website

## Audio

- Royalty-free car radio music only.
