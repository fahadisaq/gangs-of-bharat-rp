# 02. World: Mayanagri

## City name

The city is **Mayanagri** ("city of illusions"), a fictional Mumbai-inspired megacity. **DRAFT – owner review**: the city name is a working name.

## Districts

**DRAFT – owner review**: the map layout below (district list, positions and sizes) is a first proposal.

| District | Description | Main activities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slum start zone | Dense chawls and narrow lanes where every new player starts | Story intro, first jobs, cheap slum rooms, gang presence |
| Market / old city | Bazaars, old buildings, temples, mosques, street food | Shops, 24/7 stores, dhabas, clothing, pickpocket RP, police stations |
| Business district | Glass towers, banks, offices, hotels | Banks, government offices, news office, court, real estate, hotels |
| Rich hills | Bungalows and villas on a hill | Expensive houses, private garages, 5★ hotel views |
| Sea-link + beach | Sea bridge, beach promenade, sea-facing hotels | Races, nightlife, beach events, 5★ sea-facing hotel |
| Port + highway with dhabas | Container port, warehouses, long highway with roadside dhabas | Truck jobs, mafia port shipments, smuggling, fuel tankers, petrol pumps |
| Village outskirts | Fields, tractors, small village, quarry | Farmer and quarry jobs, army base area, races on village roads |

Factions have fixed headquarters (police HQ and stations, army base with weapon factory and depot, hospital, fire station, government building, news office, court, gang hideouts, mafia clubs and port warehouses).

## Transport

- **Local train** connecting districts (the intro starts with arrival by train).
- **Metro** in the business district and old city.
- **Buses** on fixed routes (also a player job: bus driver).
- Autos, taxis, bikes, cycles, cars, trucks, tractors, boats, helicopters and planes (see [07. Economy](07-economy.md) for the vehicle list).
- **Airport** with planes. Boats in the sea and port area. Helicopters, including the 5★ hotel helipad.

## Traffic and street life

- Indian-style traffic: autos, bikes, buses, trucks and cars weaving through, honking.
- **Cows on the road cannot be harmed.** Vehicles stop or bounce off them; weapons have no effect on them.
- Street food stalls, chai stalls and crowds in the market.
- Places of worship are **safe zones**: no weapons, no damage, no crime inside or in the marked area around them.

## Day, night and weather

- Day/night cycle with street lights, neon signs at night and nightlife in clubs and bars.
- **Monsoon** weather: heavy rain, wet roads (less grip), flooded low streets during monsoon season (see [06. Events](06-events.md)).

## Interiors and physics

- **Seamless interiors**: shops, houses, banks, hospital, police station and offices open without loading screens where possible.
- Car damage (dents, broken windows, smoke) and ragdoll for characters.

## New-player protection

New characters get protection from player damage and robbery for their first play hours (exact duration set during balancing), shown by an icon above the name. Protection ends early if the player attacks someone or commits a crime.
