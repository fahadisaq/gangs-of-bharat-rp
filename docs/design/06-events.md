# 06. Events

All times are server time (IST, Asia/Kolkata).

## Daily schedule

| Time | Event | Who is involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Rations convoy** | Army delivers rations; gangs may attack |
| 12:00 | **Medical supply** | Delivery to the hospital and medical stores; hospital and police |
| 14:00 | **Weapon convoy** | Army, Police, gangs (see rules below) |
| 16:00 | **Bank cash van** | Bank security and police; robbery target for gangs |
| 18:00 | **Fuel tankers** | Tankers to petrol pumps; theft **raises fuel prices** |
| 20:00 | **Prisoner transfer** | Police move prisoners; gangs may try to free them |
| 21:00 | **Weapon convoy** | Same rules as 14:00 |
| 23:00 | **Mafia port shipment** | Mafia receives goods at the port; police and other families may intercept |

Also part of daily life but not on a fixed slot: **gold transport** and **VIP escort** events, started by the event scheduler in the admin panel.

## Weapon convoy rules (14:00 and 21:00)

1. The **army weapon factory** produces weapon crates.
2. The **Police Commissioner orders crates and pays from the police budget**.
3. An **army convoy** carries the crates from the factory/depot to the **police armory**, with a **police escort**.
4. **Gangs receive a route rumor 15 minutes before** departure (approximate route, not exact).
5. **Stolen crates need 10 minutes to reach the hideout.** After a hijack the crates travel on a 10-minute delivery run to the gang hideout. Police and Army can recover them during that time; only crates that reach the hideout count as stolen.
6. The convoy only runs if **at least 5 Army and 5 Police members are online**. Otherwise it is postponed.
7. **Outcomes affect armory stock**: a delivered convoy fills the police armory; stolen crates reduce it and add illegal weapons to the gang.

## Rules for all convoys and transports

- **A gang can attack one convoy per day.**
- **Civilians are not targets.** Attacking uninvolved civilians during an event is RDM.
- Every event is logged (participants, damage, loot).

## Monthly and seasonal events

- **War games** (monthly) run by the Army.
- **Elections**: Mayor election every 30 days.
- **Festivals** (for example Diwali, Holi, Ganpati, Eid, Christmas) with decorations and special activities.
- **Monsoon season** with heavy rain and flooding.
- **Cricket** matches and screenings.
