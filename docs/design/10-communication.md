# 10. Communication

Players talk with **voice and text inside the game**. There are no real phone calls and no real SMS; everything runs through the game server.

## Channels

| Channel | Type | Range / audience | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proximity voice | Voice | Players near the speaker (fades with distance) | Free |
| Local chat | Text | Players nearby | Free |
| Faction chat | Text | All members of the player's faction, **map-wide** | Free |
| Faction radio | Voice | All members of the player's faction, **map-wide** (push-to-talk) | Free |
| Group / crew chat | Text | Members of a player-made group or crew; visible to all members | Free or a small ₹ fee (set during balancing) |
| Public / global chat | Text | Whole server, with **slow mode** (one message every few seconds) | Free |
| VIP chat | Text | VIP members only | Needs **VIP membership** |
| Ad chat | Text | Whole server, ad format | **₹ per message** (money sink; 50% to News) |
| Phone SMS | Text | One-to-one, any distance | Needs **₹ phone recharge** |
| In-game phone call | Voice | One-to-one, any distance | Uses phone recharge |
| Admin announcements | Text | Whole server | Admins only |

## Faction radio

Faction members hear their faction radio anywhere on the map, even when they are not near each other (for example an army or police unit spread across the city). Radio works only for members of that faction.

## In-game phone

- **Messages (SMS)** one-to-one, needing a ₹ **recharge** bought at 24/7 stores or through the phone.
- **In-game voice calls** between players. **DRAFT – owner review**: the owner's "not real call" was unclear; this is drafted as in-game calls (player to player through the game server, never real phone calls).
- Other apps: map, bank/UPI, faction app (hiring posts, duty), jobs, market and auction house, support tickets, news, social app, camera. **DRAFT – owner review**: the final list of phone apps.

## Moderation tools

- **Report, mute and block** on every channel and on every player card.
- **Abuse filter** on all text channels (Hindi and English word lists, plus AI voice moderation as an extra).
- **Faction leaders can mute members** in faction chat and faction radio.
- All chat is **server-logged** for reports; voice used in a report is saved from the last 2 minutes of evidence (see [12. Admin and Moderation](12-admin-moderation.md)).
