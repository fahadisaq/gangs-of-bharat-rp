# 12. Admin and Moderation

**DRAFT – owner review**: admin details (exact powers per level, staffing numbers, panel layout) are a first proposal. The owner may adjust the powers of each level.

## Player reports and support

- **Reports** from any player card or chat message. The server saves **the last 2 minutes of evidence** (positions, chat, voice, combat and transaction logs around the reported player).
- **Support tickets** in the phone app with **live admin response**, ticket **status** and a **rating** after closing.
- **One ban appeal** per ban.

## Admin levels

Admins can act **only on players and admins at a lower level**.

| Level | Title | Powers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Helper | Answer tickets and questions, see reports, warn players |
| 2 | Moderator | Level 1 + mute, kick, teleport to a player, spectate |
| 3 | Admin | Level 2 + jail, temp ban, invisible mode, live player data, handle reports |
| 4 | Senior Admin | Level 3 + long temp bans, faction issues, economy investigations, review Level 1-3 actions |
| 5 | Head Admin | Level 4 + permanent bans, pick faction heads, manage admins Level 1-4, reports against admins |
| 6 | Owner | Everything, including store control, economy settings, admin management and access to real player info |

Tools: **invisible mode**, **live spectate**, **live player data** (money, inventory, logs), **automatic hack alerts** (speed, teleport, impossible money changes).

## Anti-abuse rules for admins

- Admins appear in game **only as codes** (for example **ADM-27**), never by name.
- **No access to players' real info** (email, phone, payments) **except the Owner**, in line with the DPDP Act.
- **Separate admin accounts** from their player characters.
- **No handling of cases involving their own faction or friends.**
- **Admins cannot create items or money for themselves.**
- **Everything is logged with a reason and evidence.**
- **Reports against admins go to Level 5 or the Owner.**

## Punishment ladder

1. Warning
2. Mute
3. Kick
4. Jail (admin jail)
5. Temporary ban
6. Permanent ban

**Hacking and real-money trading = permanent ban** on the first offence (account and device ban).
