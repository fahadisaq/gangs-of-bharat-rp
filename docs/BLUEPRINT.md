## Part A. The game in one page

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **GANGS OF BHARAT** |
| Genre | Online roleplay (RP) city "live a second life" game, like Grand Mobile, Black Russia and Rio Rise, but with better graphics, a voiced story and Indian culture |
| Platforms | Android first, then iOS. High-end phones on Ultra, with lower presets so mid-range phones can play |
| City | **Mayanagri** (fictional, inspired by Mumbai): slum start zone, market and old city, business district, rich hills, sea-link bridge and beach, port and highway with dhabas, village outskirts |
| Players | **Up to 1,000 players per server.** Start with 1 server, add more as players grow |
| Servers | Each server is a fresh start: new character, level 1, no money. Progress does not carry between servers |
| Characters | One character per server. One faction at a time |
| Who runs the city | **Real players run everything**: factions, businesses, shops, hospital, police, court, media. The game only fills in when nobody is online (self-service shops) |
| Story | A ~25 minute voiced intro in Hindi and English, then story chapters released like a web series |
| Languages | Hindi and English at launch |
| Weapons | **Yes, confirmed by the owner.** Rare and serious. Legal only for Police and Army with a licence, on duty. Gangs get illegal guns through smuggling and convoy theft |
| Age rating | **18+** (guns, alcohol, tobacco, crime, voice chat with strangers) |
| Brands | Parody brands only, never real ones (Marutha, Mahindar, Tatva trucks, Ranga Rover, Mercedez, BMV, Lambor-Gini, Ferraro) |
| Release | Everything is built first, then **one full release** after private test builds and a closed beta |
| Budget | Zero 3D budget. Free tools and free tiers only |

## Part B. Every system, in plain words

### B1. Factions and leaders (chapter 04)

**Factions and seats on a 1,000-player server:**

| Faction | Seats | Salary? | Top head (picked by admins) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Police | 80 | Yes, from city treasury | Commissioner |
| Army | 60 | Yes | General |
| Hospital | 40 | Yes + 50% of treatment fees | Chief Medical Officer |
| Fire Brigade | 20 | Yes | Fire Chief |
| Government | 25 | Yes | Mayor (**elected by players every 30 days**) |
| Mass Media (TV, radio, newspaper, digital, ad agency) | 30 | Yes + 30% of ad income, shared equally and locked | Media Head |
| Court and Justice | ~15 | Yes | Chief Justice |
| Gangs (4 gangs × 40) | 160 | **No salary**, earn from crime | Bhai |
| Mafia (2 families × 30) | 60 | **No salary**, earn from smuggling and business | Don |
| **Civilians** | the rest | Jobs and businesses | none |

**How leadership works:**
- Admins pick **only the top head** of each faction, for a 30-day renewable term.
- The head appoints the leaders below. **Many people can hold the same rank** (several Colonels, several DCPs). Each leader commands **only their own unit**.
- Leaders add, remove, promote and demote **their own members**. Nobody can act on someone of equal or higher rank. Leaders promote at most to one rank below their own.
- **The head chooses powers**: a permissions screen turns 15 powers on or off per rank, or for one named person (recruit, fire, interview, spend treasury with a daily limit, start convoy, go live, sell ads, and more).
- Firing needs a reason; the fired member can appeal within 3 days. Leaders inactive for 7 days lose the post. Leaders can hold a no-confidence vote, which admins review.
- **Everything is logged.** Selling ranks for real money is a ban.

**Duty system:** faction gear works only on duty. Go to the base or station locker, tap **Start duty**, get the uniform, weapons, vehicles and salary. Tap **End duty** to hand it back and live as a civilian (civilian jobs, own business, houses, cars).

**Payday:** every 30 minutes of **active** play, never while AFK. Lawful factions are paid from the city treasury (taxes), with a minimum floor so they never collapse.

**Switching factions:** 7-day cooldown; 14 days to go from a gang to Police or Army (stops spying).

**Recruitment and interviews (lawful factions):** hiring post on the phone (Mass Media can broadcast it) → application form → automatic eligibility check → shortlist → **live voice interview at HQ** → faction test → result by SMS → 3-day probation → confirmed.

| Faction | Test |
| --- | --- |
| Police | Law quiz, driving test, shooting range |
| Army | Fitness course, shooting range, drill |
| Hospital | Medical quiz, practice revive on a dummy |
| Fire | Timed rescue drill, fire truck driving |
| Government | Interview with the mayor or a minister |
| Mass Media | Mock report or mock live interview |

**Gangs and Mafia recruit like real gangs:** invite → meet in person in their area → loyalty task → boss or right hand approves.

Full rank tables with every salary are in chapter 04.

### B2. Crime, police, court and guns (chapter 05)

- Crimes give a **wanted level of 1 to 5 stars**. Police see wanted players on their map.
- An unfair attack or kill (no RP reason) triggers an **automatic police alert**.
- Flow: **arrest → charges with evidence (alert data, witnesses, CCTV) → lawyer (a real player, or a government defender) → trial with a real judge → verdict (not guilty, fine, jail) → one appeal** for big sentences.
- Jail runs in real time, 5 to 30 minutes, with jail jobs to cut time.
- **Guns:** rare. Legal guns for Police and Army only, with a licence, on duty. Illegal guns only through gangs, smuggling and convoy theft.
- RP rules: no random killing, no metagaming, no powergaming, New Life Rule, value your life. Quitting during a chase or fight is punished.
- Character Kill (permanent death) only with the player's agreement (draft).
- No drugs: generic contraband instead (draft). Bribery allowed as RP but logged (draft).

### B3. Daily supply convoys and events (chapter 06)

**Weapon convoy, twice a day (14:00 and 21:00 server time):**
1. Army soldiers produce weapon crates at the base factory.
2. The Police Commissioner orders crates on the phone, paid from the police budget.
3. The Army loads military trucks and drives them to the police armory. Police can escort.
4. Gangs get a route rumour 15 minutes early (sometimes false) and can ambush.
5. Stolen crates must reach the gang hideout within 10 minutes; police and army can chase and recover them.
6. Needs at least 5 Army and 5 Police online. A gang can attack only one convoy per day. Civilians are never targets.

**Result matters:** delivered = bonuses and a full armory; stolen = gangs get guns and police run short until the next delivery; caught = arrests.

| Time | Daily event |
| --- | --- |
| 10:00 | Ration trucks from the village to shops and dhabas |
| 12:00 | Medical supply truck to the hospital (if stolen, healing costs more) |
| 14:00 | **Weapon convoy 1** |
| 16:00 | Bank cash van |
| 18:00 | Fuel tankers to petrol pumps (if stolen, fuel prices rise) |
| 20:00 | Prisoner transfer (gangs may try a rescue) |
| 21:00 | **Weapon convoy 2** |
| 23:00 | Mafia port shipment |

**Weekly and special:** Saturday turf war, Sunday main bank vault heist, monthly Army vs gang war games, mayor election every 30 days, Friday street race league, festivals (Diwali, Holi, Ganpati, Eid, Navratri garba, Christmas), monsoon season June to September, weekly cricket. Also gold transport heists, VIP mayor escort, a black market that moves daily, wanted posters, informers and undercover police.

### B4. Money, jobs, shops and needs (chapter 07)

- Currency: in-game **₹**. Bank keeps money safe; cash in pocket can be stolen.
- **Banks:** accounts, ATMs, UPI-style transfers, salary deposits, loans (repossession if unpaid), fixed deposits, bank robberies.
- **Civilian jobs:** dabbawala, construction, food delivery, auto driver, fisherman, bus driver, farmer, taxi, quarry, truck driver, mechanic, lawyer, real estate agent, and shop staff hired by owners.
- **Businesses: 1 per player**, bought with in-game ₹ only, limited number on the map. Chai stalls, dhabas, 24/7 shops, clothing, petrol pumps, mechanics, bars and clubs, gyms, car showroom, cinema, real estate, taxi and transport companies, port warehouse, medical store, chips-only casino (no cash-out), and **hotels from 1-star lodge to 5-star sea-facing luxury**.
  - Owners order stock (delivered by supply trucks; a robbed truck means an empty shop), hire staff, set prices within limits, pay weekly tax, and may face gang hafta. Inactive for 7 days = business goes to government auction. Unstaffed shops run self-service.
- **Needs:** hunger, thirst, energy, health, stress. Cars need fuel.
- **24/7 shop** sells snacks, water, recharge, tools, and parody cigarettes, beedi and pan masala **with health warnings** (small stress relief, long-term lower max health).
- **Bars:** drunk stages (sway, then blur and slurred voice and hard steering, then falling and blackout). Police have a breathalyzer: fine, licence suspension, car towed.
- **Vehicles:** garages, showroom, used car market, player mechanics (repairs, engine, paint, wraps, neon), damage, RTO driving licence, insurance, impound, VIP number plates, car theft (lockpick, hot-wire, alarms).

### B5. Market, auctions and houses (chapter 08)

- **Car market:** fixed-price listings; the game holds the money so nobody is scammed.
- **Auction house:** cars, houses, businesses, VIP plates, rare items. Seller minimum plus a system floor, 5% minimum bid step, 24 to 72 hours, anti-snipe (+2 minutes), bids held in escrow, 5 to 10% commission taken out of the economy.
- **Houses:** slum room, flat, bungalow, villa. Spawn point, garage, storage, furniture, weekly property tax.
- **House robbery** only when the owner is online, at least 3 police online, needs a lockpick or drill, alarm to owner and police, max 30% of storage, premium items never stolen. Owners can buy a safe, CCTV or guard dog.

### B6. Injury, ambulance and hospital (chapter 09)

| Time after going down | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0 s | Knocked down, grey screen, "Call ambulance" (108) |
| 0 to 3 min | A paramedic who arrives in time revives on the spot or takes the player to hospital |
| After 50 s | "Go to hospital" button appears |
| 3 min | Sent to hospital automatically |
| Hospital | Pay the fee, lose the cash in pocket |

Injury types: broken leg (limp), bleeding (slow health loss), gunshot (needs a doctor). Medkits fix small injuries.

### B8. Money from the game (chapter 11)

- **Premium cars bought only with real money** (coins through Google Play and Apple). The owner controls a rotating store: weekly featured cars, festival cars, limited editions (for example 50 per server), advertised on billboards, the news channel, phone notifications and showroom displays.
- Coins, monthly VIP (salary bonus, tag, free garage slot, priority queue), 60-day battle pass, VIP plates, pets.
- Premium cars resellable for in-game ₹ only, with a 10% fee (draft).
- **Fixed prices only, no loot boxes.** Follows India's 2023 Dark Patterns Guidelines and store rules. Prices are the owner's call (draft coin packs ₹79 / 159 / 399 / 799 / 1,999).

### B9. Admins and player support (chapter 12)

| Level | Title | Main powers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Helper | Tickets, warnings, 30-minute chat mute |
| 2 | Moderator | + kick, voice mute, admin jail, spectate |
| 3 | Admin | + temp bans 1 to 30 days, teleport, invisible mode, inventory and log checks, return lost items |
| 4 | Senior Admin | + permanent bans, remove faction leaders, manage levels 1 to 3 |
| 5 | Head Admin | + pick faction heads, run events, manage all admins |
| 6 | Owner | Everything: economy, store, servers |

- **Invisible admin mode:** no name, body, sound or map marker. Live spectate, live player data, automatic hack alerts.
- **Stopping admin abuse:** admins appear only as codes like "ADM-27", never see players' real info (Owner only, DPDP Act), use separate admin accounts, cannot handle cases involving their own faction or friends, cannot create items for themselves, every action logged with reason and evidence, reports against admins go to Level 5 and the Owner.
- **Player support:** report a player (the last 2 minutes are saved as evidence), help tickets in the phone with live admin replies, ticket status, rating the admin, one ban appeal.
- **Anti-hack:** the server decides money, inventory, health, damage and purchases. The phone only sends button presses. Speed and teleport checks, full transaction logs, rooted and emulator detection, account and device bans.
- **Web admin panel:** live players, tickets, bans, economy charts, store control, faction leaders, event scheduler, admin logs.

### B12. Graphics with zero 3D budget (chapter 14)

- **City:** procedural Blender scripts (buildings, roads, blocks) exported as `.fbx` for Unity.
- **Cars, characters, animations:** free CC0 models (Quaternius, Kenney, Poly Haven textures, Mixamo animations), reshaped and retextured to look Indian.
- **Special items:** free image-to-3D tools (Hunyuan3D, TRELLIS) from Gemini concept art.
- **Look:** day and night, monsoon rain and wet roads, neon at night, car damage, ragdoll.

### B13. Legal and business (chapter 16)

Register a company (for store payouts and 18% GST); India's 2025 online gaming law means **nothing can ever be cashed out** (lawyer to confirm); Terms of Service and Privacy Policy under the DPDP Act; trademark check for "Gangs of Bharat"; refund policy; 18+ age gate; human admins and community managers at launch.

**Running costs (estimate):** testing ~$0 to 20 per month; 1 server with 1,000 players ~$100 to 250 per month; 5 servers ~$600 to 1,200 per month. Oracle Cloud Always Free (Mumbai or Hyderabad) may run the first server for $0, to be tested.

| iOS builds | A cloud Mac (later) | To decide |

Not used: GitHub Actions (account billing block) and Codemagic (needs a paid Unity licence).
